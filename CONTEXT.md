# BelTransOil-Service

This context defines the public website language for BelTransOil-Service, a B2B service for international transport companies working with cashless fuel refueling and fuel cards in Belarus.

## Language

**Consultation Request**:
A message from a potential client asking BelTransOil-Service to explain how the client's company can connect to cashless truck refueling in Belarus.
_Avoid_: Lead, form submission, application
